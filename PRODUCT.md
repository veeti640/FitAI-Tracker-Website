# Lihas

## Register

brand

## Users

People who actively manage fitness, nutrition, recovery, sleep and training progress. The audience includes gym users and broader health enthusiasts who want quick, trustworthy guidance from their own health signals.

## Product Purpose

Lihas is a Finnish health intelligence product that connects health data, nutrition, recovery, training and AI coaching. The website should make the product understandable, credible and desirable, then guide visitors toward the App Store.

## Brand Personality

Athletic, intelligent and grounded. Lihas should feel precise, calm, modern and distinctly Finnish without becoming clinical or cold.

## Anti-references

Avoid hype, guilt, vague wellness language, emoji-heavy presentation, generic fitness clichés, excessive gradients, arbitrary accent colors, playful rounded typography and decorative interactions that slow the visitor down.

## Design Principles

1. Show real product evidence before making broad claims.
2. Turn complex health signals into one understandable next action.
3. Preserve the dark, layered Lihas identity while keeping every message readable.
4. Use motion to explain relationships, never merely to decorate.
5. Make the mobile experience feel deliberately composed rather than scaled down from desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain sufficient contrast, readable type, meaningful labels, usable touch targets and reduced-motion support. Avoid color-only meaning, guilt, stereotypes and gendered assumptions.
